The recovery calculation

A column, not a total. An adjudicator who cannot reconstruct the subtraction cannot check the figure, and the package they cannot check is the one they refuse rather than reconstruct. Every line below is a field of the record the gate read; the order is the order the policy applies its terms, and it is the order that decides the money. The cap is applied after the deductible — applying it to the gross would defeat the deductible entirely and claim the manufacturer's money.

The floor line is a residual, not a formula. It is the recorded recoverable amount minus what the subtractions above come to, so the column always adds up and a disagreement between the record and the arithmetic would appear here as a number rather than be hidden by re-running the calculation for display. Rounding happens once, on every field at the same moment, in recovery.finalise; every intermediate keeps full precision, because rounding at the deductible and again at the cap and again at the total drifts by a penny and kill condition F is exact Decimal equality.
