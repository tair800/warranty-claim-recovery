{% if case.truth and case.truth.outcome != case.decision.outcome.value %}

The two outcomes differ and both withhold recovery. The gate answers a missing requirement with NOT_RECOVERABLE — resubmitting now repeats the rejection the case exists to answer — while the generator's rule records absent evidence as REVIEW. The divergence is real, it is between two committed modules rather than a rendering artefact, and it is shown rather than reconciled here because reconciling it on a screen is how a screen comes to disagree with what is graded. Kill condition G counts false recoveries; a refusal recorded under the other name is not one.
